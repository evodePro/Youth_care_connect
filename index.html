Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our platform

We believe in full transparency. If your question isn't answered here, you can always chat with an advisor anonymously and they'll help you in real time.

Do I need to sign up or log in to use the chat?

No. The platform is designed specifically to remove barriers. Only our verified health advisors log in. You just choose an anonymous username, and you are instantly connected — no email, no password, no trace.

Who am I talking to? Are they real doctors?

Yes. Every advisor goes through a strict vetting process requiring a secret invite code, professional identification, and an extensive admin review before they are permitted to access the platform. They consist of Clinic Staff, Counselors, Peer Educators, and Health Advisors.

Can my school or parents see my messages?

No. Our chats are secured with enterprise-grade infrastructure using Row Level Security. Because you do not make an account, there is no email or physical identity tied to your chat history. It is completely confidential and cannot be traced back to you.

Is this service completely free?

Yes. YouthCare Connect is entirely free. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no data monetization. The platform exists to help young people access reproductive and mental health guidance without any barriers.

Can I choose which advisor I talk to?

Absolutely. Our directory page lets you browse all available advisors, filtered by specialty — Mental Health, Sexual Health, Relationships, and more. You select who you feel comfortable talking to, and the connection is made directly.
